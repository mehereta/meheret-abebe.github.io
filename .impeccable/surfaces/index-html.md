## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is Meheret's notebook, read as one big page: scrolling is a camera that zooms into each section and travels along a drawn path to the next. It refuses the stacked-section scroll and the centred hero over cards.

OWN-WORLD: White ruled notebook paper with pale blue rules and a red margin line on a blue notebook cover, blue ballpoint ink, yellow/pink/green highlighter swipes, washi tape holding photos and screenshots, yellow sticky notes for asides. Hand-drawn sketches (rough.js-roughened SVG) per section. Shantell Sans for everything.

STORY: Cover → About → Education → Experience → Projects → Leadership → Skills → Contact (the original site's section order). A visitor reads the role and email on the first screen, sees the whole notebook as a map, reads each section, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The cover framed full size: name very large, three roles in highlighter, "Email me" and résumé buttons, taped portrait, pencilled scroll hint. Adaptation of the original "whole page first" promise, citing the confirmed answer that role fit must read at a glance on the first screen: the zoomed-out map, with readable highlighter labels on each section and the dashed route, is the second stop. A full-width paper top bar holds the section links and the "Read as a list" toggle, replacing the corner strip so every section stays one click away and readable at any zoom level.

FORM: User-pinned Notebook (beats the roll). Signature interaction: scroll-driven camera with a zoom-out dip between sections, a reading pan inside tall sections with the side column following, map labels on the overview, click-to-zoom from the map, and focus moving the camera. Experience is a left-to-right timeline: an intro stop, then one camera stop per role in chronological order (NANO FAB 2021 → Neuroscience lab → TeachFX → CRAFT → CK-12), each with its own doodle, joined by the dashed route; list mode stacks the roles with dashed rules between them. Projects uses a wide layout (header row, two columns of entries). Fallback: phones, reduced motion and the list toggle render stacked pages with the current section marked in the bar. seed:afda31105679eba5
