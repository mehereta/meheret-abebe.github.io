# Portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience. One long page. Audience: edtech hiring managers first, then network. Action: reach out about a role (email), with résumé download as the secondary path. All existing content stays; copy may be tightened and typos fixed. User asked to avoid "too playful" and "too austere". Gallery (gallery.html) inherits this world.

Chosen after three rounds (assigned Montage and a bolder hand were re-rolled; picked from the safer hand).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a well-made printed course reader: a cover sheet that states the role, then tabbed dividers for each section. It refuses the centred hero over a row of equal project cards.

OWN-WORLD: Bright white sheet laid on a warm grey-beige binder board (#e9e4d8). Divider tabs in a full palette, one per section: teal Work, rust Experience, mustard Skills, blue Education, olive Leadership, plum About, ink Contact. Each section opens on a full-width divider band of its colour joined to its tab. Libre Franklin for display, tabs and labels; Literata for prose; tabular numerals for dates and figures. Hairline ink rules, no cards, no icon tiles.

STORY: Within seconds a visitor knows Meheret is a learning designer and UX researcher grounded in research. They see SyncED and the other projects as evidence, scan experience and education, meet the person and mission, and email.

FIRST VIEWPORT: White cover sheet filling most of the width on the board. Name very large top left, roles line in teal below it, one-sentence lead, a solid ink "Email me" button and a résumé link. Portrait at the right of the sheet. Tabs (Work, Experience, Skills, Education, Leadership, About, Contact) stack down the sheet's right edge as the navigation. The start of the Work divider shows at the bottom.

FORM: Course Reader, a grounded candidate from the safer re-roll (position 3 of the conventional list, picked by the user). Signature interaction: the tab for the section in view slides out from the sheet edge, and clicking a tab turns to that divider. Motion: tabs slide in from behind the sheet on load, exponential ease-out, reduced-motion safe. seed: afda31105679eba5

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
