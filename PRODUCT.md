# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

- **EdTech hiring managers** evaluating Meheret for learning design, product design, or UX research roles. They arrive from an application, a referral, or LinkedIn, skim in a few minutes, and decide whether to reach out.
- **General network**: peers, mentors, and people met at events or through Stanford who want to understand what Meheret works on and cares about.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Meheret Abebe, a Stanford M.S. Learning Design and Technology graduate (B.S. Symbolic Systems, Honors in Education). It presents Meheret's research, projects, and experience at the intersection of learning science, product design, and AI.

Success: a visitor reaches out about a role.

## Positioning

The combination is the claim: a cognitive-science and educational-neuroscience foundation (Symbolic Systems, EEG research, nanofabrication), learning-science research (honors thesis on generative AI in scenario-based learning), and shipped, user-tested products (SyncED, SMARTE, CK-12 activities). The stated mission is building education tools that help people unlock their full thinking capacity, grounded in research, informed by user testing, and rooted in constructivist learning theory.

## Operating Context

- Visitors read on desktop after clicking a link from an application or LinkedIn, and on phones after an in-person meeting.
- Hiring managers scan for role fit, evidence of process, and a way to make contact; the network reads more for story and mission.
- The resume PDF and external project links (videos, prototypes, thesis, GitHub) serve as deeper evidence.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site: `index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, and `gallery.html`, deployed on GitHub Pages.
- Single-page layout with sections: Home, About (plus Hobbies & Interests and a gallery link), Education, Experience, Projects, Leadership, Skills, Contact.
- **All current content stays.** Copy may be tightened and corrected (the source has typos such as "eduaction", "diffrentiated", "scalabity", "Mulivariable"), but no section or project is dropped.
- Structure, layout, and visual identity are otherwise open; nothing in the current look is binding.
- Gallery images (`IMG_7458.HEIC`, `IMG_7417.HEIC`, `IMG_1362.HEIC`) are HEIC, which most non-Safari browsers do not display. They need conversion to a web format for the gallery to work.
- Image assets in the repo root are large (several 2–5 MB JPGs and PNGs) and unoptimized.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Meheret Abebe. Voice is first-person, warm, and mission-driven ("My mission is simple yet ambitious…").
- Footer line "Yes I built this website myself!" is part of the existing voice.

## Product Principles

1. **Lead with role fit.** A hiring manager should know within seconds what kind of role Meheret is for and how to get in touch.
2. **Show process, not just output.** Research, user testing, and learning-science grounding are the differentiator; projects should make that visible.
3. **Keep the person in it.** Mission, cultural roots, and leadership history are part of the story for both audiences, not filler.
4. **Every claim is real.** Use only the evidence on hand; tighten copy, never inflate it.
